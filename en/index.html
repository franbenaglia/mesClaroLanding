MesClaro

Your spending, under control

A monthly expense planner for Android, private and fully offline.

After paying everything I have planned, how much money do I really have left this month?

MesClaro does more than log transactions: it separates what you already have, what you still have to pay or receive, and the balance you would end the month with.

Coming soon to Google Play Read the privacy policy

What it does

Everything you need to plan the month, with no accounts and no bank connections.

  • Projected free money

    The dashboard shows how much you would have left at the end of the month, next to your current and projected balance.

  • Paid and pending

    Every income or expense can be done or planned. Upcoming payments are marked as paid with one tap.

  • Recurring expenses

    Rent, salary or subscriptions are added automatically every month or every year, never twice.

  • Budgets with alerts

    A total budget, a savings goal and a limit per category, with clear alerts at 80% and 100%.

  • Monthly statistics

    Spending by category, day-by-day progress, comparison with the previous month and exceeded limits.

  • Your data is yours

    Export transactions to CSV and save a full backup to a file, wherever you choose.

Private by design

Your financial data never leaves your phone. This is not just a promise: the app cannot connect to the Internet.

  • No Internet permission
  • No account or sign-up
  • No ads
  • No analytics or trackers
  • No bank or card connections
  • No cloud backups
